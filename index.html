About Me

I’m a PhD student in the College of Information Science and Electronic Engineering at Zhejiang University, where I work in the NICE Lab under the supervision of Prof. Rongpeng Li. I received my bachelor’s degree from the College of Electronics and Information Engineering at Sichuan University in Chengdu, China.

My current research focuses on large language model alignment, reinforcement learning, and LLMs and RL for communication networks.
