About Me

I’m a PhD student in the College of Information Science and Electronic Engineering (ISEE) at Zhejiang University (ZJU). I work in the NICE Lab with Prof. Rongpeng Li. In 2024, I earned my B.E. from the College of Electronics and Information Engineering at Sichuan University (SCU) in Chengdu, China.

My current research interests include Large Language Model (LLM) Alignment, Reinforcement Learning (RL), and LLMs and RL for Communication Networks. I enjoy exploring questions across these fields, especially with collaborators who approach them from a different angle.

Outside the lab, I like to lift weights, play guitar, and shoot hoops.
